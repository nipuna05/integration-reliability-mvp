# Customer Interview Guide (20-30 min)

Goal: confirm the problem is real, costly and already being paid for. Do not pitch first.

## Opening
"We are researching how companies keep connected systems (HR, payroll, CRM, finance) accurate. Can I ask about your experience?"

## Questions
1. Which systems exchange data in your business? Who owns each integration?
2. Tell me about the last time an integration failed or data was wrong. How was it found?
3. How long between the failure and someone noticing? What did it cost (hours, money, customers)?
4. How do you check integrations today? (manual, tests, monitoring tools, nothing)
5. What do you spend on this today (people time, tools, consultants)?
6. Who would approve budget to fix this?
7. What stops you from solving it now?
8. If a tool or service caught these failures within minutes, what would that be worth?

## Close
- Can we show you a 10-minute demo on a similar scenario?
- Who else should we talk to?

## Record after each call
Company, role, systems involved, last incident, cost, current tooling, budget owner, interest (1-5), next step.

## Signals to proceed
3+ interviews with a recent costly incident, existing spend, and a reachable budget owner.
